# math5465-spam-email

* Caps lock
* Key word (free, winner, prize, etc.)
* Urls (www.)
* Containing special characters
* Long (above a certain number of characters)
* Discrepencies between email and message


### KNN:
- Splits csv file into true or false (ham or spam) then measures the conditions listed above. Then to determine if an email is spam or not it finds five near neighbors (similar conditions met),
  based on what the neighbors are classified as, determines what the email is classified as.
